# Last Jump Home (c8 · The Mother of All)

**For:** chapter 8, The Mother of All, the finale. The spores, their mother, the grappler, tides and herds, then the
leviathan itself: bigger than the ship, and not hunting us but sick. Blast what's eating it off its hide and set it
free. After that the ship jumps on its own.
**Mood:** the last stand, and then mercy. Huge and dark, ending in release instead of victory. (The current synth
piece's identity: a ticking clock, the theme on a music box, a talking growl bass, half-time drums.)

## Mission shape (about 8:20 to the jump, the music runs to about 8:50)
The encounters are evenly spaced, then the boss fight:
- 0:30: spores. 1:00: the spore mother (an early big moment).
- 1:40: the grappler.
- 2:10-4:00: herds and tides, rising steadily.
- 4:30: **the leviathan arrives.** The biggest drop of the piece, the theme at full size.
- 4:30-8:00: the fight. Phases, with a held-breath breakdown when it breathes out (everyone stays inside), and a last
  push when the engine override comes into play.
- About 8:10: it's freed. A cutscene, then the ship jumps by itself at about 8:20. The ending should turn from fight
  to release: a majestic, hopeful major chord, not a victory stomp.

## Style of Music
```
epic hybrid orchestral and bass music instrumental, 112 BPM half-time, C minor resolving to C major, opens with a ticking clock and a lone music box melody, talking growl bass, heavy half-time drums with a huge snare on 3, taikos, full orchestra with brass and low strings, 808 sub drops, the music box theme returns over the climax, monstrous and tragic, final section turns majestic and merciful, ends on one enormous C major chord ringing out, no vocals
```
