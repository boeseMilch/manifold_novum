# Keep S-7 Frozen (c3 · The Empty Lab)

**For:** chapter 3, The Empty Lab: Dr. Varga's research module drifts dark and airless. Get inside, find the black box
and carry it home. The freezer log says KEEP S-7 FROZEN, and something in there is still alive.
**Mood:** a derelict, quiet and wrong. Exploration in the dark that turns into a hunt.

## Mission shape (5:00 to the jump, the music runs to about 5:30)
The mines opener arrives at 0:15-0:30, then the derelict docks at about 1:00 (the quiet exploring part, 1:00-2:00).
After that comes a slow climb, the hot times around 3:45 and 4:45, and the jump at 5:00.
