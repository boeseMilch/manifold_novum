# Red Comet Gallop (c4 · Red Comet Cove)

**For:** chapter 4, Red Comet Cove: pirates took Dr. Varga. Get a nav core from one of the Red Comet gang's raiders,
either by boarding one or by fishing it out of space, and install it in the helm.
**Mood:** pirates, the jolly rowdy kind, as a swashbuckler score: a boarding party, a stolen chart, a tavern brawl in space. (The old synth piece's identity, kept as the colour: a 6/8 jig, accordion, fiddle, stomps and crew shouts.)

## Mission shape (5:00 to the jump, the music runs to about 5:30)
The interceptor opener arrives at 0:15-0:30, then a calm valley from about 1:00 to 1:20, then raiders and boarding
from about 1:10 to 2:40. The hot times are around 3:45 and 4:45, and the jump is at 5:00.

## Style of Music
```
cinematic pirate adventure instrumental, 6/8, 126 BPM, A minor, a swashbuckling hybrid orchestra with fiddle and accordion as the colour, solo cello theme, driving low string ostinato, stomps and claps, taikos and struck metal pipe percussion, sub pulse like the ship's heartbeat, crew shouts HEY on the drops, brass charge at the climax, rowdy and fun, ends on one loud A major chord with a final stomp, instrumental apart from the shouts
```

## Exclude Styles
```
sung lyrics, lead vocals, EDM, dubstep, irish pub band, bells, music box, ambient
```
