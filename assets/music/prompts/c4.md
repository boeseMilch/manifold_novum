# Red Comet Gallop (c4 · Red Comet Cove)

**For:** chapter 4, Red Comet Cove: pirates took Dr. Varga. Get a nav core from one of the Red Comet gang's raiders,
either by boarding one or by fishing it out of space, and install it in the helm.
**Mood:** pirates, but the jolly, rowdy kind. A boarding party, a stolen chart, a tavern brawl in space. (The current
synth piece's identity: a 6/8 jig, accordion, fiddle, stomps and crew shouts.)

## Mission shape (5:00 to the jump, the music runs to about 5:30)
The interceptor opener arrives at 0:15-0:30, then a calm valley from about 1:00 to 1:20, then raiders and boarding
from about 1:10 to 2:40. The hot times are around 3:45 and 4:45, and the jump is at 5:00.
