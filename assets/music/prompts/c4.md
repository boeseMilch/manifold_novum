# Red Comet Gallop (c4 · Red Comet Cove)

**For:** chapter 4, Red Comet Cove: pirates took Dr. Varga. Get a nav core from one of the Red Comet gang's raiders,
either by boarding one or by fishing it out of space, and install it in the helm.
**Mood:** pirates, as a swashbuckler score: a boarding party, a stolen chart, a fight in space. Bold and a bit fun, but the same mysterious, epic sound as the rest. (Rewritten 2026-11-07, like c1 and c2: the core of the liked songs, piano, orchestra, a wordless choir, good drums and a synth bass, with the old colour (the 6/8 swing, a far-away fiddle) kept only as a hint.)

## Mission shape (5:00 to the jump, the music runs to about 5:30)
The interceptor opener arrives at 0:15-0:30, then a calm valley from about 1:00 to 1:20, then raiders and boarding
from about 1:10 to 2:40. The hot times are around 3:45 and 4:45, and the jump is at 5:00.

## Style of Music
```
epic cinematic adventure instrumental, hybrid orchestra and synth, 6/8, 126 BPM, A minor, a driving grand piano ostinato in 6/8, galloping low string ostinato, a low male wordless choir chanting on ah, growl synth bass on the drops, punchy drums and taikos, struck metal pipe percussion, a solo fiddle melody far in the background in the calm parts, brass charge at the climax, bold and swashbuckling, dark but fun, wide reverb, ends on one loud A major chord, no lyrics
```

## Exclude Styles
```
lyrics, sung words, lead vocals, shouts, rap, spoken word, sea shanty, irish pub band, folk, accordion, EDM, dubstep, bells, music box, ambient
```
