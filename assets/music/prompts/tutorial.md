# Basic Training (tutorial)

**For:** the solo tutorial (`tutorial`, js/tutorial.js): RAIDER basic training, one station at a time, with pauses for
the texts.
**Mood:** friendly, curious, a bit cheeky. A drill instructor who likes you. The score's chamber side: strings and struck pipes, played lightly. It sits under reading and clicking, so it must stay out of the way and loop.

## Style of Music
```
playful cinematic chamber instrumental, a friendly training montage in space, 100 BPM, C major, pizzicato strings, clarinet and bassoon melody, solo viola answers, light marching snare, ticking struck metal pipe percussion, soft ticking pulse, cheeky and curious, steady energy, no build, loopable, ends on the same chord it starts on, no vocals
```

## Exclude Styles
```
vocals, choir, EDM, drops, epic, dark, horror, heavy drums, bells, music box, marching band brass
```

## Lyrics (Instrumental off, tags only)
```
[Intro: ticking pipes and pizzicato, soft pulse]
[Theme: clarinet melody over pizzicato strings]
[Verse: solo viola takes the tune, marching snare]
[Theme: clarinet and bassoon together, the viola under them]
[Bridge: pizzicato strings alone, tiptoeing]
[Theme: the whole little ensemble]
[Outro: back to ticking pipes and pizzicato, same chord as the start]
```

## Notes
- A loop: 2-3 minutes. Keep it light: the texts and sound effects must stay readable over it.
- Download the **stems** into `assets/music/Basic Training/`.
