# Basic Training (tutorial)

**For:** the solo tutorial (`tutorial`, js/tutorial.js): RAIDER basic training, one station at a time, with pauses for
the texts.
**Mood:** friendly, curious, a bit cheeky. A drill instructor who likes you. It sits under reading and clicking, so it
must stay out of the way and loop.

## Style of Music
```
playful instrumental marching band in space, 108 BPM, C major, pizzicato strings, bassoon and clarinet melody, tuba bass, light marching snare and woodblock, cheeky and curious, like a friendly training montage, steady energy, no build, loopable, ends on the same chord it starts on, no vocals
```

## Exclude Styles
```
vocals, choir, EDM, drops, epic, dark, drone, bells, horror, heavy drums
```

## Lyrics (Instrumental off, tags only)
```
[Intro: marching snare and tuba]
[Theme: clarinet melody over pizzicato strings]
[Verse: bassoon takes the tune, woodblock ticks]
[Theme: clarinet and bassoon together]
[Bridge: pizzicato strings alone, tiptoeing]
[Theme: full little band]
[Outro: back to snare and tuba, same chord as the start]
```

## Notes
- A loop: 2-3 minutes. Keep it light: the texts and sound effects must stay readable over it.
- Download the **stems** into `assets/music/Basic Training/`.
