# Basic Training (tutorial)

**For:** the solo tutorial (`tutorial`, js/tutorial.js): RAIDER basic training, one station at a time, with pauses for
the texts.
**Mood:** friendly and curious: the first steps on the ship, one station at a time. The score's light side: piano, pizzicato and a soft choir, played lightly. It sits under reading and clicking, so it must stay out of the way and loop. (Rewritten 2026-11-07, like c1 and c2: the core of the liked songs, piano, orchestra, a wordless choir, good drums and a synth bass, with the old colour kept only as a hint.)

## Style of Music
```
light mysterious cinematic instrumental, curious first steps on a starship, 100 BPM, C minor lifting to C major, a bright grand piano ostinato, pizzicato strings, solo viola melody, a soft wordless choir on ooh in the background, gentle ticking struck metal pipe pulse, soft round synth bass, light tight half-time drums, curious and friendly, steady energy, no build, loopable, ends on the same chord it starts on, no lyrics
```
