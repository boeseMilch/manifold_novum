# Bloom (c6 · Spore Season)

**For:** chapter 6, Spore Season: drilling samples of the living rock in the Mire Nebula as it drifts past, one per
crew member, while leeches, spore clouds and a spore mother notice.
**Mood:** alive and strange. Beautiful and slightly sickening, like a nebula that breathes: the score turned organic. (The old synth piece's identity, kept as the colour: a breathing wordless choir, marimba in 3 against 4, hand drums.)

## Mission shape (5:00 to the jump, the music runs to about 5:30)
The leeches open at 0:15-0:30, the rock drifts past from about 1:00 (drilling, back and forth), then a slow climb. The
spore mother and the swarm come in the middle, the hot times are around 3:45 and 4:45, and the jump is at 5:00.
