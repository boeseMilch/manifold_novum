# Outlaws of the Kessler Lanes (c1 · First Contract)

**For:** chapter 1, First Contract: the first job in the new (used) ship. Pirate raiders rob freighters in the Kessler
lanes, and the Tollgate Trade Guild pays a bounty on each one.
**Mood:** a space western. Bounty hunters in a rattling ship, cocky and a bit comic, with a real showdown at the end.

## Mission shape (5:00 to the jump, the music runs to about 5:30)
The first encounter arrives at 0:10-0:30, then a calm valley from about 1:00 to 1:20, a slow climb, and the hot times
around 3:45 and 4:45 (about 15 s after the encounters arrive). The jump is at 5:00, and the music should still be rising
there. It needs calm sections, two climaxes and a clear final hit.

## Style of Music
```
spaghetti western instrumental in space, 96 BPM, E minor, twangy reverb baritone guitar, whistled melody, harmonica, galloping drums with tom rolls, whip cracks and anvil hits, trumpet solo at the climax, cocky outlaw swagger, starts sparse with a lone guitar, builds to a big showdown, wide cinematic reverb, ends on one huge E major hit, no vocals
```

## Exclude Styles
```
vocals, choir, EDM, dubstep, drone, bells, horror, ambient pad intro
```
