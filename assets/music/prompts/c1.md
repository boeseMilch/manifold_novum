# Outlaws of the Kessler Lanes (c1 · First Contract)

**For:** chapter 1, First Contract: the first job in the new (used) ship. Pirate raiders rob freighters in the Kessler
lanes, and the Tollgate Trade Guild pays a bounty on each one.
**Mood:** a space western told by the ship's orchestra: bounty hunters in a rattling ship, cocky and a bit comic, with a real showdown at the end.

## Mission shape (5:00 to the jump, the music runs to about 5:30)
The first encounter arrives at 0:10-0:30, then a calm valley from about 1:00 to 1:20, a slow climb, and the hot times
around 3:45 and 4:45 (about 15 s after the encounters arrive). The jump is at 5:00, and the music should still be rising
there. It needs calm sections, two climaxes and a clear final hit.

## Style of Music
```
cinematic space western instrumental, Morricone over a hybrid orchestra, 92 BPM, E minor, twangy reverb baritone guitar and harmonica as the colour, solo cello theme, low string ostinato, galloping half-time drums and toms, anvil and struck metal pipe hits, deep sub drops, trumpet solo and a distant wordless choir at the showdown, cocky swagger, starts sparse with a lone guitar, builds to a big showdown, wide reverb, ends on one huge E major chord, no lyrics
```

## Exclude Styles
```
lead vocals, lyrics, sung words, whistling, EDM, dubstep, country pop, banjo, bells, music box, ambient pad intro
```
