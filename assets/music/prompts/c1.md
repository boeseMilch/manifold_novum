# Outlaws of the Kessler Lanes (c1 · First Contract)

**For:** chapter 1, First Contract: the first job in the new (used) ship. Pirate raiders rob freighters in the Kessler
lanes, and the Tollgate Trade Guild pays a bounty on each one.
**Mood:** the first job, out into the unknown: mysterious and a little cocky, then epic. (Rewritten 2026-11-06: the first take as a Morricone space western didn't fit the score. The western survives only as a far-away tremolo guitar; the rest is the core of the liked songs: piano, orchestra, wordless choir, punchy drums, synth bass.)

## Mission shape (5:00 to the jump, the music runs to about 5:30)
The first encounter arrives at 0:10-0:30, then a calm valley from about 1:00 to 1:20, a slow climb, and the hot times
around 3:45 and 4:45 (about 15 s after the encounters arrive). The jump is at 5:00, and the music should still be rising
there. It needs calm sections, two climaxes and a clear final hit.

## Style of Music
```
mysterious epic cinematic instrumental, hybrid orchestra and synth, 92 BPM, E minor, a lone grand piano motif in wide reverb, warm analog synth bass ostinato pulsing in eighths, deep sub drops, solo cello theme, a distant wordless choir on ooh and aah, tight punchy half-time drums with big toms, struck metal pipe accents, low string ostinato, a trumpet call at the showdown, a hint of tremolo baritone guitar far in the background, starts sparse and curious, builds to a huge epic climax with the full orchestra and choir, ends on one huge E major chord, no lyrics
```

## Exclude Styles
```
lyrics, sung words, lead vocals, rap, spoken word, country, western, harmonica, whistling, banjo, EDM, dubstep, bells, music box, ambient pad intro
```
