# Strange Cargo (c2 · Strange Cargo)

**For:** chapter 2, Strange Cargo: a delivery job, two sealed salvage pods for the Xenobiology Institute. They're warmer
than cargo should be, and around 2:40 whatever is inside breaks out. The frigate comes as the finale, about 70 s
before the jump.
**Mood:** a cargo run that turns strange: calm and curious, then eerie as the pods wake up, then a chase and an epic fight with the frigate. (Rewritten 2026-11-06: the first take as a 1950s B-movie with theremin and surf guitar didn't fit the score. The calm-to-eerie turn stays; the sound is now the core of the liked songs: piano, orchestra, wordless choir, punchy drums, synth bass.)

## Mission shape (5:00 to the jump, the music runs to about 5:30)
The swarm opener arrives at 0:15-0:30, then a calm valley from about 1:00 to 1:20. The creepy turn comes at about
2:40-2:55, when the pods break open. The hot times are around 3:45 (the frigate arrives at about 3:50) and 4:45, and
the jump is at 5:00.

## Style of Music
```
mysterious dark cinematic instrumental, hybrid orchestra and synth, 100 BPM, C minor, a soft felt piano motif with celesta and vibraphone shimmer, curious and calm at first, a solo violin, a wordless female choir on ooh drifting in like something breathing, then eerie and wrong with a low choir hum, slow heartbeat sub pulse, then a driving chase: gritty saw synth bass ostinato, staccato strings, punchy half-time drums with big toms and struck metal pipes, brass hits and the choir at full size at the climax, epic, wide reverb, ends on one loud C major chord, no lyrics
```

## Exclude Styles
```
lyrics, sung words, lead vocals, rap, spoken word, theremin, surf guitar, swing, jazz, lounge, EDM, dubstep, bells, music box, trailer
```
