# Strange Cargo (c2 · Strange Cargo)

**For:** chapter 2, Strange Cargo: a delivery job, two sealed salvage pods for the Xenobiology Institute. They're warmer
than cargo should be, and around 2:40 whatever is inside breaks out. The frigate comes as the finale, about 70 s
before the jump.
**Mood:** a 1950s B-movie played by the ship's orchestra. It starts as a jaunty, slightly too relaxed cargo run, turns creepy, then into a monster chase and a fight with the frigate.

## Mission shape (5:00 to the jump, the music runs to about 5:30)
The swarm opener arrives at 0:15-0:30, then a calm valley from about 1:00 to 1:20. The creepy turn comes at about
2:40-2:55, when the pods break open. The hot times are around 3:45 (the frigate arrives at about 3:50) and 4:45, and
the jump is at 5:00.

## Style of Music
```
cinematic sci-fi mystery instrumental, 1950s b-movie colour over a hybrid orchestra, 100 BPM, C minor, theremin melody, pizzicato strings and vibraphone, walking bass, starts jaunty and too relaxed, turns eerie with the theremin wavering over a solo cello, then a frantic chase with driving string ostinato, half-time drums and twangy surf guitar, struck metal pipe percussion, sub pulse like the ship's heartbeat, big brass hits at the climax, ends on one loud C major chord, no vocals
```
