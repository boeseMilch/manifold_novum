# A Signal in the Dark (c5 · Rescue Dr. Varga)

**For:** chapter 5, Rescue Dr. Varga: deal with the Red Comet's patrol, dock with their rusty base, fight through it
room by room, patch Varga up and cover her run back to our ship, then detach and jump.
**Mood:** a rescue. Her faint distress signal out in the dark, a tense raid, then a heroic run home: the most classically heroic piece of the score. (Rewritten 2026-11-07, like c1 and c2: the core of the liked songs, piano, orchestra, a wordless choir, good drums and a synth bass, with the old colour (the radio, the far-away flugelhorn) kept only as a hint.)

## Mission shape (7:00 to the jump, the music runs to about 7:30)
Every encounter keeps its written time here:
- 0:25: the patrol (a short early fight, 0:30-0:55).
- 1:00: we dock. From about 1:00 to 4:00, the room-by-room raid: tense, steady, slowly building.
- 2:30-2:50: a second patrol hits while we're docked.
- 3:50-4:10: mines.
- From about 4:30, Varga is freed and runs for our ship under fire. This is the big heroic climax.
- 5:00-6:00: a breather once she's aboard, while the crew detaches.
- 6:00-7:00: the escape, rising into the jump at 7:00.

## Style of Music
```
mysterious epic cinematic rescue instrumental, hybrid orchestra and synth, 92 BPM, C minor lifting to Eb major at the climax, opens with a distant crackling radio and a lone piano motif like a distress signal, a flugelhorn answers far away, solo violin, a wordless choir on ooh and aah, pulsing sub synth bass and 808 hits, punchy half-time drums, driving spiccato strings, low toms and struck metal pipe percussion for the raid, heroic brass theme and the choir at full size at the climax, hopeful and tense, wide reverb, ends on one big Eb major chord with the piano on top, no lyrics
```

## Exclude Styles
```
lyrics, sung words, lead vocals, rap, spoken word, EDM, dubstep, rock, drone intro, bells, sonar, music box
```
