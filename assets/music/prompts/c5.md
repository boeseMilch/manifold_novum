# A Signal in the Dark (c5 · Rescue Dr. Varga)

**For:** chapter 5, Rescue Dr. Varga: deal with the Red Comet's patrol, dock with their rusty base, fight through it
room by room, patch Varga up and cover her run back to our ship, then detach and jump.
**Mood:** a rescue. Her faint distress ping out in the dark, a tense raid, then a heroic run home: the most classically heroic piece of the score. (The old synth piece's identity: the theme on a far-away radio horn, a voice answering, warm strings, spiccato strings.)

## Mission shape (7:00 to the jump, the music runs to about 7:30)
Every encounter keeps its written time here:
- 0:25: the patrol (a short early fight, 0:30-0:55).
- 1:00: we dock. From about 1:00 to 4:00, the room-by-room raid: tense, steady, slowly building.
- 2:30-2:50: a second patrol hits while we're docked.
- 3:50-4:10: mines.
- From about 4:30, Varga is freed and runs for our ship under fire. This is the big heroic climax.
- 5:00-6:00: a breather once she's aboard, while the crew detaches.
- 6:00-7:00: the escape, rising into the jump at 7:00.

## Style of Music
```
cinematic rescue score instrumental, hybrid orchestra, 92 BPM, C minor lifting to Eb major at the climax, opens with a distant crackling radio and a lone flugelhorn melody, a solo violin answers it, deep sub bass and 808 hits, driving spiccato strings, low toms and struck metal pipe percussion for the raid, half-time drums, snare marches, heroic brass theme and a wordless choir at the climax, hopeful and tense, wide reverb, ends on one big Eb major chord with the flugelhorn on top, no lyrics
```
