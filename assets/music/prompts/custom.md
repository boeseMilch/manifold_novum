# Pick Your Trouble (custom skirmish)

**For:** the custom skirmish (`custom`): no story and no pay, just the encounters the host picked, spread over five
minutes. Hold out until the jump.
**Mood:** an arena. Pure action and no narrative, modern and punchy.

## Mission shape (5:00 to the jump, the music runs to about 5:30)
The usual shape: something arrives at 0:10-0:30, a short calm from about 1:00 to 1:20, a climb, the hot times around
3:45 and 4:45, and the jump at 5:00.

## Style of Music
```
hard-hitting drum and bass instrumental, 172 BPM with half-time sections, G minor, Reese bass, chopped breakbeats, glitchy stutters, filtered synth stabs, clean sub drops, starts with filtered drums only, half-time calm section, builds into full-speed rolling breaks, two big drops, arena energy, ends on one huge impact and sub boom, no vocals
```

## Exclude Styles
```
vocals, choir, orchestral, folk, jazz, drone intro, bells
```
