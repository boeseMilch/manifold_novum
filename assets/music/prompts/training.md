# Side by Side (crew training)

**For:** crew training (`training`, js/tutorial-mp.js): the multiplayer tutorial, where everyone learns every station
at once, side by side, and nothing pauses.
**Mood:** a crew getting in sync. Busy, upbeat, everyone moving at once, a team that's starting to click: the score at its most driving, without building. (Rewritten 2026-11-07, like c1 and c2: the core of the liked songs, piano, orchestra, a wordless choir, good drums and a synth bass, with the old colour kept only as a hint.)

## Style of Music
```
driving cinematic instrumental, a crew getting in sync, 112 BPM, E minor, punchy tight drums with ghost notes, rolling analog synth bass in sixteenths, repeating grand piano ostinato, staccato string ostinato, solo violin riff, short wordless choir stabs on ah, brass stabs, metal clank percussion, low engine pulse, busy and upbeat but a little mysterious, steady groove, no big build, loopable, ends on the same chord it starts on, no lyrics
```

## Exclude Styles
```
lyrics, sung words, lead vocals, rap, spoken word, EDM drops, dubstep, epic trailer, horror, bells, music box, lo-fi hip hop, ballad, disco, funk guitar
```
