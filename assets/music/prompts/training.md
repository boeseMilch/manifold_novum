# Side by Side (crew training)

**For:** crew training (`training`, js/tutorial-mp.js): the multiplayer tutorial, where everyone learns every station
at once, side by side, and nothing pauses.
**Mood:** a crew getting in sync. Busy, upbeat, everyone moving at once, a team that's starting to click.

## Style of Music
```
upbeat instrumental funk, 114 BPM, E minor, tight drums with ghost notes, slap bass, clavinet riff, wah guitar, punchy horn section stabs, a crew working together in sync, busy but light, steady groove, no big build, loopable, ends on the same chord it starts on, no vocals
```

## Exclude Styles
```
vocals, choir, EDM, drops, orchestral, dark, drone, bells, horror, ballad
```

## Lyrics (Instrumental off, tags only)
```
[Intro: drums and slap bass]
[Groove: clavinet riff joins]
[Theme: horn section melody]
[Verse: wah guitar and clavinet trade licks]
[Break: drums and bass only, two bars]
[Theme: horns again, everyone in]
[Outro: back to the opening groove, same chord as the start]
```

## Notes
- A loop: 2:30-3:30. It must sit under several people talking.
- Download the **stems** into `assets/music/Side by Side/`.
