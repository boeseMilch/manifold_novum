# Side by Side (crew training)

**For:** crew training (`training`, js/tutorial-mp.js): the multiplayer tutorial, where everyone learns every station
at once, side by side, and nothing pauses.
**Mood:** a crew getting in sync. Busy, upbeat, everyone moving at once, a team that's starting to click: the score as a heist-movie groove.

## Style of Music
```
upbeat cinematic heist groove instrumental, a crew getting in sync, 112 BPM, E minor, tight funky drums with ghost notes, groovy electric bass, staccato string ostinato, solo cello riff, punchy brass stabs, struck metal pipe percussion, sub pulse like the ship's heartbeat, busy but light, steady groove, no big build, loopable, ends on the same chord it starts on, no vocals
```

## Exclude Styles
```
vocals, choir, EDM, drops, dark, horror, ballad, bells, music box, disco
```
