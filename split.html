SPLIT SCREEN

Up to four players on one screen, in the same game: two side by side, three or four in quarters. Each picks a name and what they play with: the keyboard and mouse (one player), or a controller each. Progress (money, gear, locker, chapters) is kept by name; the name you usually play as here keeps its usual save.

Controllers show up once you press a button on them. The first view (top left) plays the sound and shows the mission plates and messages, once for everyone. Back to the normal game
